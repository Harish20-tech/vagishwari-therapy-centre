Home Visit Therapy in Chennai

Speech therapy, occupational therapy, swallowing therapy and special education in the comfort of your own home, for children and adults who find it hard to travel to the centre.

Call 90804 62913 WhatsApp us

Who is home visit therapy for?

Home visits are helpful for adults recovering from stroke, older adults, children who are more comfortable at home, and families who find travel difficult.

Our therapists bring the activities and materials needed and use everyday routines at home, which makes it easier for families to continue practice between sessions.

Therapies available at home

Speech therapy

For children and adults, including speech after stroke.

Occupational therapy

Sensory, motor and daily living skills.

Swallowing therapy

Safe swallowing and feeding support at home.

Special education

Learning support for children.

Autism support

Communication and play skills in a familiar setting.

Home visits may suit you if

  • Travel to the centre is difficult
  • The person is recovering from stroke or illness
  • Your child is calmer and learns better at home
  • You want therapy built around daily routines

Is home therapy as effective as therapy at the centre?

Yes, for many people. Therapy at home also helps families see and practise strategies in daily routines.
