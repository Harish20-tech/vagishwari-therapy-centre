Care for every stage of life

Every plan begins with a careful assessment and is built around the person, not a template.

"The first word is worth the wait. And the work."

For children

Playful, structured sessions for children who are late to talk, hard to understand, or finding it hard to connect through words. Parents learn simple activities to continue at home.

  • Delayed speech
  • Articulation
  • Receptive language
  • Expressive language
  • Stammering
  • Autism communication
"Find your voice again, at your own pace."

For adults

Therapy for voice problems, stammering, and speech difficulties after a stroke or other neurological condition, focused on everyday communication and confidence.

  • Voice disorders
  • Vocal hygiene
  • Stammering & fluency
  • After stroke
  • Neurological speech
  • Confidence building
"Clear speech begins with clear hearing."

For hearing

Hearing tests and screening for all ages, with the results explained in plain language and clear guidance on what to do next.

  • Hearing tests
  • Hearing screening
  • Hearing loss
  • Hearing aid guidance
  • Counselling
  • Progress reviews
"Small hands, growing skills, bigger independence."

Occupational therapy

Play-based occupational therapy that helps children build the motor, sensory and self-care skills they need at home and in school, with simple routines for parents to follow at home.

  • Fine motor skills
  • Handwriting readiness
  • Sensory processing
  • Gross motor & balance
  • Daily living skills
  • Attention & sitting tolerance
"Every child learns. Some just need a different path."

Special education support

Support for children with learning difficulties and autism, building communication, attention and everyday learning skills, with guidance for parents and teachers.

  • Learning difficulties
  • Autism support
  • Attention & focus
  • Social communication
  • School readiness
  • Parent guidance

Questions parents often ask

Is my child too young for speech therapy?

No. Early support usually makes the biggest difference. If your child isn't meeting the milestones above, or you simply have a feeling something isn't right, an assessment is a good first step at any age.

Will they "just grow out of it"?

Some children do catch up, but many need a little help. An assessment tells you which, so you aren't left waiting and wondering.

Do you see adults and seniors too?

Yes. We work with adults on voice, stammering and speech after a stroke or other neurological condition, and offer hearing assessments for all ages.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Any earlier medical, hearing or school reports, and a short note of what you've noticed and when it started. For children, a favourite toy can help them settle in.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes. Home visits are available for children and adults who find it difficult to travel to the centre. Call us to arrange one.

Are you open on Sundays?

Yes, but on Sundays sessions are by appointment only. Please call or WhatsApp us in advance to book a Sunday slot.

How do I book?

Tap "Book an appointment" to call or WhatsApp us on 90804 62913 or 99406 69998, and we'll fix a time that suits you.
