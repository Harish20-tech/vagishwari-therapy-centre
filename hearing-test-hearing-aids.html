Why get your hearing tested?

Hearing loss often comes on slowly, so people may not notice it themselves. Family members may notice the TV getting louder or questions being missed. In children, hearing problems can delay speech and affect learning.

A hearing test tells you whether there is a hearing loss, what type it is and how much. If a hearing aid will help, we let you try one before you decide, and we fit and adjust it for you.

Signs you or a family member may have hearing loss

  • Turning the TV or phone volume up high
  • Often asking people to repeat themselves
  • Difficulty hearing in noisy places
  • Ringing or buzzing in the ears
  • A child who does not respond to name or sounds
  • Speech delay in a young child

Frequently asked questions

How long does a hearing test take?

A routine hearing test usually takes about 30 to 45 minutes, and results are explained straight away.

Can I try a hearing aid before buying?

Yes. We offer a hearing aid trial so you can hear the difference before deciding.

Can young children have their hearing tested?

Yes. We offer hearing screening for babies and young children, which is important if speech is delayed.
