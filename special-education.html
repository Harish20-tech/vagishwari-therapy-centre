What is special education?

Special education is teaching planned around how an individual child learns. Some children find reading, writing, numbers, attention or following classroom routines harder than their classmates. With the right methods, step-by-step goals and plenty of encouragement, they can learn with confidence.

Every child starts with an assessment of their strengths and needs. We then set clear goals, share them with parents and, where helpful, guide teachers on how to support the child at school.

Special education support we offer

Learning difficulties

Support for children who struggle with reading, writing, spelling or early maths, including signs of dyslexia.

Autism support

Structured, visual teaching to build learning, communication and social skills.

ADHD & attention

Strategies to improve focus, sitting tolerance, task completion and following instructions.

School readiness

Pre-academic skills, classroom routines and independence for starting or returning to school.

Individual education plans

Clear, measurable goals for each child, reviewed regularly with parents.

Parent & teacher guidance

Practical ideas so the same approach continues at home and in the classroom.

Signs your child may need special education support

  • Falling behind classmates in reading or writing
  • Difficulty remembering letters or numbers
  • Trouble paying attention in class
  • Not following instructions or routines
  • Avoiding homework or getting very frustrated
  • Diagnosed with autism, ADHD or developmental delay
  • Teachers reporting learning concerns
  • Needs help getting ready for school

Special education works well alongside occupational therapy and speech therapy, which are also available at the centre.

Frequently asked questions

What is special education?

Special education is teaching that is planned around how an individual child learns. It supports children who find learning, attention or communication harder than others, using structured, step-by-step methods and visual supports.

Which children benefit from special education?

Children with learning difficulties such as dyslexia, autism, ADHD, developmental delay, slow learning or difficulties with attention and school readiness often benefit. An assessment helps decide what support is needed.

Can special education be combined with speech therapy and occupational therapy?

Yes. Many children make the best progress when special education, speech therapy and occupational therapy work together. All three are available at Vagishwari Speech and Hearing Centre.

Do you help children who are already in school?

Yes. We support school-going children with reading, writing, early maths, attention and classroom skills, and can guide parents and teachers on how to help at school and home.
