How we support children with autism

Every child with autism is different. We start with a detailed assessment of communication, play, sensory needs and learning, and then build a plan around your child.

Therapy focuses on understanding and using language, requesting and expressing needs, play and social interaction, attention and daily routines. Parents learn strategies to use at home so progress continues between sessions.

Autism therapy services we offer

Speech and communication therapy

Building words, sentences, gestures and alternative ways to communicate.

Social and play skills

Eye contact, turn-taking, shared attention and playing with others.

Occupational therapy

Sensory regulation, motor skills and daily living skills.

Special education

School readiness, early academics and learning support.

Behaviour and routines

Visual schedules and strategies for calmer daily routines.

Parent guidance

Practical coaching so families can support progress at home.

Early signs of autism parents notice

  • Limited eye contact or response to name
  • Late talking or loss of words already learned
  • Repeating words or phrases instead of conversation
  • Preferring to play alone or lining up toys
  • Strong reactions to sounds, textures or changes in routine
  • Repetitive movements such as hand-flapping or spinning

Frequently asked questions

Can speech therapy help a child with autism?

Yes. Speech therapy helps children with autism understand language, communicate their needs and interact with others. Earlier support usually leads to better progress.

Do you offer OT and special education for autism too?

Yes. Speech therapy, occupational therapy and special education are all available at our centre, so your child can be supported in one place.

Do you diagnose autism?

A formal diagnosis is made by a developmental paediatrician or psychiatrist. We assess communication and learning needs and can guide you on next steps.
